## I can’t see the overlay

1. Open Yaplay and press **Start**.
2. Check that you chose the correct display.
3. Join a call and enable your client’s Yaplay integration.

The overlay stays invisible until the integration sends call data. Installing Yaplay alone does not connect it to Yapin.

## My client won’t connect

Use `ws://127.0.0.1:8080` and run both apps on the same computer. If you changed the port in Yaplay, change it in the integration too.

If the port is already in use, close any other Yaplay instance or choose another port in both apps.

## The call buttons are disabled

Your integration must support overlay controls, and you must be in a call. Check with the integration’s developer if the roster works but the controls don’t.

## Notifications don’t appear

Enable notifications in Yaplay’s settings and save. Notifications sent while disconnected are not replayed; visible notifications disappear after eight seconds.

## macOS won’t open the app

The builds are not notarized. For a build you trust, check **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Make sure you downloaded the right version for your Mac’s chip.

## The shortcut doesn’t work

Use **Cmd+Shift+Y** on macOS or **Ctrl+Shift+Y** on Windows. Check whether another app already uses that shortcut.

## Still stuck?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ilyeshdz/yaplay/issues) with your OS, Yaplay version, client integration, and steps to reproduce the problem. Integration developers can also check the [protocol](/docs/protocol) and [connection lifecycle](/docs/lifecycle).
