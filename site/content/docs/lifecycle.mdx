---
title: "Wire a plugin lifecycle"
---

Use these lifecycle steps in your Yapin client modification’s plugin system, whether you are targeting Yapmod, Yap++, or another host. The exact hooks depend on the host.

1.  **Enable:** read your integration’s port setting, create one client, subscribe to the host’s Yapin events, capture the current call/mic/deafen state, then connect.
2.  **Update:** publish the full roster on relevant changes. Coalesce rapid speaking events instead of sending a message for every audio sample. Keep IDs stable and mark only the local member as `is_self`.
3.  **Reconnect:** keep your cached state current while disconnected. Publish roster first, then mic and deafen. Do not replay historical notifications.
4.  **Leave call:** send `in_call: false` with an empty roster. The provided wrapper also removes cached mic/deafen state.
5.  **Disable or unload:** unsubscribe all host listeners, send the end-call message if your integration owns the overlay, then disconnect to cancel retry timers.

A connection closing does not automatically clear the overlay because other clients may be connected. A crash or abrupt shutdown can therefore leave the last roster visible. The clear-on-unload message is best effort, not guaranteed delivery.

### Check your integration

*   Connect with Yaplay running, and also start Yaplay after your plugin.
*   Join a call, update speaking/mute state, add and remove members, then leave.
*   Restart Yaplay during a call and verify the current state returns.
*   Disable your plugin and verify it stops reconnecting.
*   Try a custom port and disabled notifications.
*   Verify actual microphone behavior in Yapin; Yaplay is only the display.
