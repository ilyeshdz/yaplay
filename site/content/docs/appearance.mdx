---
title: "Customize the overlay"
---

By default, press **Cmd+Shift+Y** on macOS or **Ctrl+Shift+Y** on Windows, then select the settings gear. Choose **Appearance** in the sidebar for colors, sizing, and roster details. Choose **Overlay** for the display and panel placement. Choose **Privacy** for connection notices. The preview updates while you edit; **Save** applies the settings to the running overlay and remembers them for next launch. Closing the settings window discards unsaved edits.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Compact rows | Smaller avatars and less vertical space. |
| Scale | 75–150% for text and avatars. |
| Panel width | 180–400 logical pixels, scaled and constrained to the screen. |
| Roster corner | Top left, top right, bottom left, or bottom right. Choosing a corner resets the saved voice and notification positions. |
| Background opacity | 0–100%; text and avatars stay readable at full opacity. |
| Corner radius | Square through rounded, 0–24 pixels. |
| Screen margin | 8–80 pixels from the chosen edges. |
| Maximum visible people | 1–20, reduced further when the screen is too short. |
| Profile pictures, names, heading, yourself | Show or hide each detail. At least pictures or names remain visible. |
| Active speakers first | Speakers first, then yourself, then the remaining roster in source order. Disable to preserve roster order. |
| Background, text, speaking highlight | Any six-digit `#RRGGBB` color. |

**Move panels freely** makes the voice panel and notifications interactive so you can drag them anywhere on any connected display; the controls have a drag grip and can always be moved. Positions save automatically and are restored on the next launch, per display. Choosing a roster corner or changing the display resets the voice and notification positions.

**Reset appearance** restores the defaults in the form; press Save to apply them. Old settings files receive the new defaults automatically.

## Large calls

Outside arrangement mode the voice panel is click-through. It displays only the configured number of people that fit vertically, with a **+N more** count for the rest. Active speakers are promoted into view when speaker prioritization is enabled. Names stay on one line and truncate with an ellipsis. Only visible rows load profile pictures.

Notifications are their own click-through window, positioned from the opposite corner by default. They use the same colors, opacity, scale, and corners, and show only as many cards as fit. Long notification text is truncated. Roster and notification widths are constrained to separate halves of the screen. With no notifications, choose **Move panels freely** to drag the notification placeholder.

## Speaker-only roster

Turn on **Speakers only** in **Appearance → Roster details** to show people while they speak. **Keep after speaking** keeps their row visible for another 0–10 seconds (2 by default). The speaking highlight turns off as soon as they stop. The panel disappears when the last visible speaker's delay expires. **Show yourself**, speaker priority, and the maximum visible count still apply.

## Notifications

In **Overlay → Notifications**, choose a display time from 1–30 seconds and a maximum of 1–5 cards. Turn on **Titles only** for shorter cards that omit the message body. Saving a shorter duration or smaller limit also updates cards already on screen. Defaults are eight seconds, five cards, and full messages.

## Client connections

In **Privacy → Connections**, turn off **Accept connections** to refuse new integrations and disconnect the current client. The setting applies when you press **Save**; until then the existing connection keeps working. While connections are blocked, **General** shows **Connections paused** instead of the listening port.

In **Privacy → Connection awareness**, turn off **Notify when a client connects** to hide the card shown whenever an integration connects to Yaplay. The active client still appears in **General**. Leaving the notice on is recommended so unexpected connections are easier to spot.

## Global shortcuts

In **General → Global shortcuts**, select a shortcut and press the new combination. Include a modifier key. If the system intercepts the combination before Yaplay receives it, select **Type instead**, enter it (for example, `Ctrl+Shift+M`), and select **Use shortcut** to check availability. **Save** activates your choices.

Mute and deafen shortcuts are optional. They send the same requests as the control panel during a call with a compatible integration. Select **Clear** and save to release either shortcut.

## Profile pictures

Turn **Profile pictures** on to show avatars supplied by your integration. Missing or unavailable pictures fall back to initials. Turn the option off to show names instead.

Choose your monitor in **Display**. If it is disconnected, Yaplay uses the primary display.

Developers can find avatar requirements in the [protocol reference](/docs/protocol).
